Almost there

The trust profile is downloading — allow it, then two quick steps in Settings.

  1. 1
    Tap Allow when Safari asks about the configuration profile.
    Didn't see it? Download again
  2. 2
    Open the Settings app and tap the Janus local HTTPS profile — under Profile Downloaded near the top, or General → VPN & Device Management — then Install.
  3. 3
    Still in Settings: General → About, scroll to Certificate Trust Settings, and switch on the certificate named Caddy Local Authority. (The step everyone misses.)
  4. ✓
    Go back to your browser — it notices within seconds and takes you to the status page, lock and all.
iOS discards a downloaded profile after 8 minutes — if you wandered off, download again.
